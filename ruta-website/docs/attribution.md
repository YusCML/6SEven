## Replacing them

If the team shoots or licenses its own photography, drop the files in under the
same names and update this table — no component changes are needed.

## Why JPEG for photos

Photographs are stored as `.jpg` and flat graphics as `.png`. PNG is lossless
and would make these files several times larger for no visible gain; PNG is kept
for the map, avatar and logos, where flat colour compresses well and edges must
stay crisp.
